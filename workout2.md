### Rules

- **Fixed wake, flexible bed.** Same 06:30 all seven days. Weekends may drift by
  one hour at most, at both ends.
- **Weekend ceiling: bed 23:30, wake 07:30.** More than that is social jetlag and
  you pay for it on Monday.
- **Sunday night returns to weekday bed.** Monday is decided Sunday evening.
- **Caffeine cutoff 13:00** (14:00 on weekend days).
- **2 h minimum between dinner and bed.** That is exactly why Tue/Thu bed is not
  earlier than 22:45.
- **No hard training after 20:30.** Runs finish at 20:30.
- **Short night (<6.5 h):** 20 min nap or NSDR before 15:00. Do not sleep in to
  compensate, and do not push the wake later.
- **Room:** dark, cool (18–20 °C), phone charging outside the bedroom.

**Why this is a priority:** two weeks of sleep extension in short sleepers cut
objective intake substantially and lowered body weight with no change in energy
expenditure (Tasali et al., *JAMA Internal Medicine* 2022). Evidence for intake is
strong; for long-term weight it is mixed.

## Daily walk — every day

- **15 min outdoors within 30 min of waking.** Daylight, moderate pace.
- **10 min after lunch** on weekdays.
- **Step floor: 8,000 a day, counted wake to sleep** (target 9,000–10,000). Phone in
  your pocket counts it.
- **That is a whole-day total, not a target for the 15 minutes.** Morning walk gives
  ~1,500–2,000 and the lunch walk ~1,000–1,200; the rest is ordinary movement —
  stairs, errands, standing calls. On a desk-bound day, 8,000 means about 20 extra
  minutes of moving *somewhere in the day*, never a longer morning walk.
- **Pace matters:** at least **2,500 steps brisk** (≥100 steps/min, you can talk but
  not sing). Step goals alone changed no body composition in an RCT; steps plus
  intensity did.

## Tracking and checkpoint

Three numbers only. More tracking is not better tracking.

| Metric | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Weight** | Mon / Wed / Sat, on waking | After toilet, before food or water, same scale. Record all three; use the **weekly average** |
| **Waist at navel** | Sunday morning | Measure 3×, keep the middle value |
| **Steps** | Daily | Today's total from your phone; note any day under 8,000 |

Optional: one line per lift (load × reps) so progression is a decision, not a guess.

**Checkpoint: Sunday 22 November, evening.** Nothing changes before then — not the
food, not the training, not the sleep target.

**If the weekly average is flat for two full weeks** (Sun 22 and Sun 29), change
**one** thing: trim 40 g cooked rice on two more days, **or** add 1,000 steps. Not
both. Never below week 1's food.

**Expect week 2 to look flat on the scale.** Daily walking holds water transiently,
and Thursday's dinner lands close to bed. Judge at the checkpoint, not on Sunday.

## If the week collapses

2 lifts + 1 run. Skip Thursday before Wednesday. Never skip both lifts.
On a bad day, the minimum is a 10 min walk and the lifts — food stays as written,
no improvised snacks.

## Stop signals

Resting HR +5 bpm for 3 days · sleep latency past 45 min · strength down two
sessions running · joint ache past warm-up.

→ Drop the morning circuit first, keep the walks. If it persists, hold volume a week
instead of pushing through.
