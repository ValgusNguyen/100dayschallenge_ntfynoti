# Week 1 — Fat Loss + Muscle Plan


Goal: lose fat steadily (~0.3–0.5 kg/week) while keeping and building muscle.
No breakfast. Two real meals + one snack, all inside a **12:00–20:00** window
(**12:00–21:00** on run days — Tue/Thu).
Every dish is ≤15 min with prepped ingredients.

> **Weights:** meat/fish/veg are **raw** weights. Rice/noodles are **cooked** weights unless marked "dry".
> Calories are estimates (±10%). Use a food scale for the first 1–2 weeks, then use the hand guide.

## Sunday meal prep (60–75 min)

1. **Rice (10 min active):** cook 2.5–3 cups brown rice. Portion 120–170 g; freeze 4 portions, fridge 3.
2. **Chicken (25 min):** poach 1.2 kg breast with ginger, lemongrass, a pinch of salt. Shred half, slice half; portion 150–200 g.
3. **Beef (15 min):** split ~850 g into 5–6 freezer bags. Marinades:
   - 2 bags: minced lemongrass + fish sauce + garlic + pepper
   - 1 bag: five-spice + garlic + fish sauce (bò lúc lắc)
   - 2 bags: garlic + shallot + fish sauce + black pepper
   Freeze. Thaw in the fridge the night before.
4. **Eggs (10 min):** boil 10 eggs.
5. **Tofu (5 min):** portion ~600 g; keep in water in the fridge, change water every 2 days.
6. **Veg (20 min):** wash and cut everything; store in boxes with a paper towel.
7. **Sauce (5 min):** jar of fish sauce + lime + garlic + chilli + water — **no sugar**.
8. **Salmon:** freeze 3 portions (~150 g each); thaw overnight. Cook fresh 10–12 min (or bake 2 portions Sunday).
9. **Snacks:** portion 7 × 200 g yogurt and the fruit so you don't improvise.

**Weekday cooking:** nothing takes more than 15 min. Beef stir-fry 7 min, salmon 10–12 min, soup 10 min, chicken is already cooked.

## Nutrition + training rules

- **Eat in this order:** vegetables → protein → carbs. It lowers the glucose spike from rice/noodles.
- **Protein at every meal:** 40–50 g at lunch/dinner, ~20 g at the snack. This protects muscle in a deficit and keeps you full.
- **Low-GI carbs:** brown rice and sweet potato are the default. Bún/phở appear only on training days (Mon/Tue/Thu/Sat) and in the portions listed.
- **Salmon 3×/week** for omega-3 — helpful for inflammation and insulin sensitivity.
- **Sugar:** no sugary drinks, no sweetened yogurt. Whole fruit max 2 servings/day. Nước chấm without sugar.
- **Sodium:** fish sauce is salty — dilute it, use lime/chilli/garlic for flavour, and drink water.
- **Strength 2×/week + daily walking** is your insulin-sensitivity lever. Don't skip Strength A/B.
- **Sleep:** wake 06:30 daily · bed 22:30 (22:45 Tue-Thu, 2 h after dinner) · weekend max +1 h (bed 23:30, wake 07:30), Sunday night back to normal · caffeine cutoff 13:00.
- **Weigh once a week** (same day, morning, after toilet, before food). Track the weekly average, not daily fluctuations.
- **Hungry?** Add vegetables and protein, not rice. Don't eat back workout calories.
- If weight is unchanged for 3 weeks, reduce rice by ~50 g/day or add 1,000 steps — **don't drop below 1,500 kcal**.
- This is general nutrition guidance, not medical advice. If you take metformin/other medication or have other conditions, check with your doctor or dietitian.
