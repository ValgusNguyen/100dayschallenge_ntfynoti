## Sleep schedule

**Wake 07:00 every day.** That is the anchor — you already wake 07:00–07:30, so this
week stops fighting it. Bedtime follows from the wake time, not the other way round.

| Night | Bed | Wake | In bed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon / Tue / Wed / Thu night | **23:15** | 07:00 | 7 h 45 |
| Fri → Sat | 00:00 | 08:00 | 8 h |
| Sat → Sun | 00:00 | 08:00 | 8 h |
| Sun → Mon | **23:15** | 07:00 | 7 h 45 |

**The ladder:** 23:15 this week → **22:45 week 4** → **22:30 week 5**. You were at
midnight; 23:15 is a 45-minute step, which is the size that holds. Do not jump to
22:30 in one move.

### Rules

- **Fixed wake, flexible bed.** Same 07:00 all seven days. Weekends may drift by one
  hour at most, at both ends.
- **Weekend ceiling: bed 00:00, wake 08:00.** More than that is social jetlag.
- **Sunday night returns to weekday bed.** Monday is decided Sunday evening.
- **Caffeine cutoff 13:00** (14:00 on weekend days).
- **2 h minimum between dinner and bed.** Tue/Thu dinner at 20:45, bed 23:15 — that
  is exactly 2 h 30.
- **No hard training after 20:30.** Runs finish at 20:30.
- **Short night (<6.5 h):** 20 min nap or NSDR before 15:00. Do not sleep in to
  compensate, and do not push the wake later.
- **Room:** dark, cool (18–20 °C), phone charging outside the bedroom.

**Why this is a priority:** two weeks of sleep extension in short sleepers cut
objective intake substantially and lowered body weight with no change in energy
expenditure (Tasali et al., *JAMA Internal Medicine* 2022). Evidence for intake is
strong; for long-term weight it is mixed. You are at ~7 h, which is adequate — the
target is consistency, not more hours.

## Daily walk — the actual week-3 lever

Week 2 averaged **5,613 steps/day** with **one** day over 8,000 and **Wednesday at
1,656**. That is the number this week exists to fix.

- **15 min outdoors at 07:15 — Tue / Wed / Thu only.** Daylight, moderate pace.
  These are the three mornings you have; no walk on the other four.
- **20 min after lunch — Mon–Fri.** This is the Wednesday repair: 1,656 + morning
  walk + lunch walk + normal movement is how Wednesday gets off the floor.
- **Step floor: 7,000 whole-day total. Tue/Wed/Thu: 9,000. Sunday: 5,000 minimum.**
- **That is a whole-day total, not a target for the 15 minutes.** The morning walk
  gives ~1,500–2,000 steps; the lunch walk ~2,000–2,200. The rest is ordinary
  movement — stairs, errands, standing calls.
- **Pace matters:** at least **2,500 steps brisk** (≥100 steps/min, you can talk but
  not sing). Step goals alone changed no body composition in an RCT; steps plus
  intensity did.

## Lift block + Chloe Ting — Mon / Wed

**Order matters: the 12 minutes of lifting happens first, then the video.**

| Day | Exercise | Sets × Reps |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Goblet squat | 3 × 8 |
| Mon | One-arm dumbbell row | 3 × 10 per side |
| Mon | Push-up | 3 × 8 |
| Wed | Hip thrust or glute bridge | 3 × 8 |
| Wed | Lat pulldown or band row | 3 × 10 |
| Wed | Overhead press | 3 × 8 |

Then **Chloe Ting, 15–20 min** of the current day's session. Stop the video at 20
minutes even if it runs longer.

**Progression — load, not volume:** when the top set hits the top of its rep range
cleanly, add **2.5 kg** (or 2 reps on push-up). RPE 7. If form breaks, hold the
weight and repeat it.

**Why the lift block stays:** a fixed video has no load variable, and progressive
resistance is what protects lean mass in a deficit. Two to four sessions per week
produce comparable strength and body-composition outcomes in women (systematic
review and meta-analysis, 2026). Twelve minutes buys the progression; the video is
the part that keeps you showing up.

**Never add a third lifting day.**

## If the week collapses

2 lifts + 1 run. Skip Thursday before Wednesday. Never skip both lifts.

On a bad day, the minimum is **the 12 min lift block + a 10 min walk**. Food stays
as written, no improvised snacks.

## Stop signals

Resting HR +5 bpm for 3 days · sleep latency past 45 min · strength down two
sessions running · glute or hamstring soreness that outlasts the warm-up.

→ Cut the Chloe Ting video to 10 min first, keep the lift block and the walks. If it
persists, hold volume a week instead of pushing through.
