# Week 3 — Fat Loss + Muscle Plan

Goal: hold fat loss at **0.3–0.5 kg/week** by rebuilding daily movement and sleep,
not by cutting food. **One dish a day, cooked once, eaten twice** — lunch at 12:00,
dinner inside the window. No breakfast.

Week 1 worked (−1 kg after 3–4 weeks of gaining). Week 2 held the food but the
walking collapsed: **5,613 steps/day average, one day over 8,000, Wednesday at
1,656.** Week 3 therefore changes **movement, sleep and cooking format**. The food
totals are week 2's, to the gram.

> **Weights:** meat, fish and veg are **raw** weights. Rice is **cooked** weight
> (150 g lunch / 120 g dinner), batch-cooked Sunday. Use a food scale for the first
> 1–2 weeks, then the hand guide.

2 fists = veg · 1 thumb = oil/nuts.

## Sunday meal prep (60 min)

1. **Rice (10 min active):** cook ~700 g dry brown rice → ~1.9 kg cooked.
   Portion **7 × 150 g** and **7 × 120 g**; freeze 8, fridge 6.
2. **Protein (20 min):** portion and freeze shrimp 550 g, squid 600 g, pork loin
   500 g, minced pork 300 g, mackerel 400 g, salmon 500 g, chicken 450 g. Thaw in
   the fridge the night before.
3. **Tofu (5 min):** 750 g firm tofu, keep in water, change water every 2 days.
4. **Eggs (10 min):** boil 3.
5. **Veg (20 min):** wash and cut bok choy, celery, onion, shiitake, cải ngọt,
   winter melon, mồng tơi, broccoli, carrot, cabbage, cucumber. Boxes with paper
   towel. Keep **lá chanh** and **thì là** whole and dry.
6. **Aromatics (5 min):** shallot, garlic, ginger, chilli; one jar of fish sauce +
   lime + chilli (no sugar).
7. **Snacks:** portion 7 × 200 g yogurt and the fruit.

**Weeknight cooking:** nothing over 20 min. Rim 8 min · stir-fry 4 min · kho 12 min ·
steam 18 min · foil bake 15 min.

## Nutrition + training rules

- **Eat in this order:** vegetables → protein → carbs. It lowers the glucose spike from rice.
- **Protein at every meal:** 45–65 g at lunch, 45–50 g at dinner, ~20 g at the snack.
  Two meals from one dish must still clear **45 g each** — check the box before you
  close it.
- **Low-GI carbs:** brown rice is the default; bún/phở only on training days if you
  swap rice out, in the same gram range.
- **Oily fish 2×/week** (mackerel Thu, salmon Sat). Shrimp, squid and snakehead are
  lean — they are protein, not omega-3.
- **Pork is loin only.** Belly and shoulder are a different food; they are not on
  this list.
- **Sugar:** none in the kho dishes or nước chấm — coconut water, fish sauce, lime,
  chilli, garlic only. No sugary drinks, no sweetened yogurt. Whole fruit max 2
  servings/day.
- **Stevia:** allowed, 1–2 servings/day, in coffee/tea or nước chấm. It replaces
  sugar; it never gets added to a meal that had none. Not in yogurt.
- **Sodium:** fish sauce is salty — dilute it, use lime/chilli/garlic, drink water.
- **Strength 2×/week + daily walking** remains the main lever. Do not skip the 12 min
  lift block; it is the part that protects muscle.
- **Sleep:** wake **07:00** daily · bed **23:15** this week · weekend max +1 h
  (bed 00:00, wake 08:00), Sunday night back to normal · caffeine cutoff 13:00.
- **Weigh 3×/week** (Mon/Wed/Sat, same conditions). Track the **weekly average**,
  never a single number.
- **Hungry?** Add vegetables and protein, not rice. Don't eat back what the workout
  burned.
- **One variable at a time.** Movement, sleep and cooking format are this week's
  variables. Food holds.
- This is general nutrition guidance, not medical advice. Your doctor is managing
  your care; if they advise differently, follow them.

bún chả (grilled pork) are good options — keep the portion to one palm of protein
and one fist of carbs.
