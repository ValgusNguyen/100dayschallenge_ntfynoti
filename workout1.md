## Sleep schedule

**Wake 06:30 every day.** That is the anchor — bedtime follows from it, not the
other way round.

| Night | Bed | Wake | In bed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon / Wed / Fri / Sat / Sun night | 22:30 | 06:30 | 8 h |
| Tue / Thu night (run days) | 22:45 | 06:30 | 7 h 45 |
| Fri → Sat | 23:30 | 07:30 | 8 h |
| Sat → Sun | 23:30 | 07:30 | 8 h |
| Sun → Mon | 22:30 | 06:30 | 8 h |

### Daily rhythm

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 06:30 | Wake · water · 10–20 min walk in daylight within 30 min |
| 12:00 | First meal — window opens |
| 17:00 | Snack (18:00 on Tue/Thu, pre-run) |
| 18:30 | Strength A/B (Tue/Thu: run 19:45–20:30) |
| 19:30 | Dinner (20:45 on Tue/Thu) |
| 20:00 | Last food — Mon/Wed/Fri/Sat/Sun |
| 21:00 | Last food — Tue/Thu |
| 21:45 | Screens down, lights dim |
| 22:30 | Bed (22:45 Tue/Thu) |

### Rules

- **Fixed wake, flexible bed.** Same 06:30 all seven days. Weekends may drift by
  one hour at most, at both ends.
- **Weekend ceiling: bed 23:30, wake 07:30.** More than that is social jetlag and
  you pay for it on Monday.
- **Sunday night returns to 22:30 / 06:30.** Monday is decided Sunday evening.
- **Caffeine cutoff 13:00** (14:00 on weekend days, 9 h before a 23:30 bed).
- **2 h minimum between dinner and bed.** That is exactly why Tue/Thu bed is
  22:45 and not 22:30.
- **No hard training after 20:30.** Runs finish at 20:30, so 22:45 clears the
  core-temperature drop.
- **Short night (<6.5 h):** 20 min nap or NSDR before 15:00. Do not sleep in to
  compensate, and do not push the wake later.
- **Room:** dark, cool (18–20 °C), phone charging outside the bedroom.

## Strength A — Monday

Supersets: alternate the pair, 60–90 s rest between pairs.

| Pair | Exercise | Sets × Reps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Squat (goblet or back) | 3 × 6–8 |
| 1 | Push-up or bench press | 3 × 6–10 |
| 2 | Romanian deadlift | 3 × 8 |
| 2 | One-arm dumbbell row | 3 × 8–10 per side |
| 3 | Farmer carry | 3 × 30 m |
| 3 | Dead bug | 3 × 10 per side |

## Strength B — Wednesday

| Pair | Exercise | Sets × Reps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hip thrust or glute bridge | 3 × 8–10 |
| 1 | Overhead press | 3 × 6–10 |
| 2 | Split squat | 3 × 8 per leg |
| 2 | Lat pulldown or assisted pull-up | 3 × 8–10 |
| 3 | Face pull | 3 × 12–15 |
| 3 | Side plank | 3 × 30 s per side |

Warm-up: 6 min easy cardio + 1 ramp-up set on the first lift.
RPE 7–8. Add 2.5 kg when the top set hits the top of its rep range cleanly.

## Runs (3 km loop, 19:45–20:30)

**Tuesday — intervals:** 6 min warm-up → 6 × 1 min brisk (RPE 7–8) / 90 s easy →
3 min cool-down.

**Thursday — easy Zone 2:** 30 min at 6–6.5 km/h, conversational, nose breathing
possible. Never hard.

Distance stays 3 km indefinitely. Only the Tuesday interval count grows.

## If the week collapses

2 lifts + 1 run. Skip Thursday before Wednesday. Never skip both lifts.

## Stop signals

Resting HR +5 bpm for 3 days · sleep latency past 45 min · strength down two
sessions running · joint ache past warm-up → hold volume, do not push through.
