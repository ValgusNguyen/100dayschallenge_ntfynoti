# Week 2 — Fat Loss + Muscle Plan


Goal: hold fat loss at **0.3–0.5 kg/week**,  by adding
daily walking and a real bedtime, not by cutting food further. No breakfast.
Two real meals + one snack, all inside a **12:00–20:00** window (**12:00–21:00** on
run days — Tue/Thu). Every dish is ≤15 min with prepped ingredients.

Week 1 worked: −1 kg after 3–4 weeks of gaining. Week 2 changes **dishes**, not
structure. Food is nearly identical to week 1; the walking is the actual change.

> **Weights:** meat/fish/veg are **raw** weights. Rice/noodles are **cooked** weights unless marked "dry".
> Use a food scale for the first 1–2 weeks, then use the hand guide.

## Sunday meal prep (60–75 min)

1. **Rice (10 min active):** cook 2.5–3 cups brown rice. Portion 120–150 g; freeze 4 portions, fridge 3.
2. **Chicken (25 min):** poach 1.2 kg breast with ginger, lemongrass, a pinch of salt. Shred half for phở, slice half; portion 180–200 g.
3. **Beef (15 min):** split ~900 g into 5–6 freezer bags.
   - 2 bags: minced, lemongrass + chilli + garlic + fish sauce (bún bò xào)
   - 2 bags: minced, five-spice + garlic + fish sauce (lá lốt, trứng hấp)
   - 1 bag: sliced, garlic + shallot + fish sauce (bò xào bông cải)
   Freeze. Thaw in the fridge the night before.
4. **Eggs (10 min):** boil 10 eggs.
5. **Tofu (5 min):** portion ~1.1 kg; keep in water in the fridge, change water every 2 days.
6. **Fish:** portion basa 200 g, tilapia 220 g, mackerel 180 g, salmon 3 × 150–160 g. Freeze all but the first two days; thaw overnight.
7. **Veg & herbs (20 min):** wash and cut everything; store in boxes with a paper towel. Keep **lá lốt** and **thì là** whole and dry — they bruise if chopped.
8. **Marinade (5 min):** one jar of sả-ớt-garlic-fish sauce (no sugar); keep lime and chilli separate for nước chấm.
9. **Snacks:** portion 7 × 200 g yogurt and the fruit so you don't improvise.

**Weekday cooking:** nothing takes more than 15 min. Kho dishes 12 min, fish 10–12 min, stir-fry 7 min, soup 10 min, chicken is already cooked.

## Nutrition + training rules

- **Eat in this order:** vegetables → protein → carbs. It lowers the glucose spike from rice/noodles.
- **Protein at every meal:** 40–50 g at lunch/dinner, ~20 g at the snack. This protects muscle in a deficit and keeps you full.
- **Low-GI carbs:** brown rice and sweet potato are the default. Bún/phở appear only on training days (Tue/Thu/Sat) and in the portions listed.
- **Oily fish 4×/week** (salmon Tue/Thu/Sun, mackerel Sat) for omega-3.
- **Sugar:** none in the kho dishes or nước chấm — coconut water, fish sauce, lime, chilli, garlic only. No sugary drinks, no sweetened yogurt. Whole fruit max 2 servings/day.
- **Stevia:** allowed, 1–2 servings/day, in coffee/tea or nước chấm. It replaces sugar; it never gets added to a meal that had none. Not in yogurt, not as a daily soft drink.
- **Sodium:** fish sauce is salty — dilute it, use lime/chilli/garlic for flavour, and drink water.
- **Strength 2×/week + daily walking** is your insulin-sensitivity lever. Don't skip Strength A/B.
- **Sleep:** wake 06:30 daily · bed **22:45** this week, **22:30 from week 3** · weekend max +1 h (bed 23:30, wake 07:30), Sunday night back to normal · caffeine cutoff 13:00.
- **Weigh 3×/week** (Mon/Wed/Sat, same conditions). Track the **weekly average**, never a single number.
- **Hungry?** Add vegetables and protein, not rice. Don't eat back what the workout burned.
- **One variable at a time.** Any change is held 3 weeks before the next one.
- This is general nutrition guidance, not medical advice. Your doctor is managing your care; if they advise differently, follow them.
